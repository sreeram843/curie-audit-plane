\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Office,}

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, ``A Privacy-Preserving Provenance
Plane for Reconstructable Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources to Large
Language Model Clinical Artificial Intelligence Transactions,'' as a Regular
Paper in IEEE J-BHI.

The work studies reconstructable provenance for one bounded synthetic
FHIR-to-LLM clinical-AI transaction without storing hidden chain-of-thought
or raw protected health information in an immutable log. The evaluation is an
engineering study on synthetic fixtures, not a clinical-efficacy trial.

This manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. A
related J-BHI manuscript by the same author on retrieval-augmented FHIR
translation addresses a different research question.

No human subjects or identifiable clinical records were used.
