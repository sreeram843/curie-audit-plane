# CMPB submission requirements

**Target:** *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine* (Elsevier)

**Status:** Working submission note; re-check the CMPB Guide for Authors and
Editorial Manager immediately before submission.

CMPB publishes computing methods and software systems for biomedical research
and medical practice, including application-software design in biomedical
informatics. Curie Audit Plane is a plausible fit as a reconstructable
provenance method and system demonstration on synthetic FHIR-to-LLM fixtures,
not as a clinical-efficacy study.

- Journal: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computer-methods-and-programs-in-biomedicine
- Editorial Manager: https://www.editorialmanager.com/cmpb/

## Article type

Prepare an **Original Research Manuscript**.

## Format (confirm live GFA before upload)

- Structured abstract ≤350 words with headings Background and Objectives;
  Methods; Results; Conclusions.
- Main text normally ≤3500 words excluding the abstract.
- Numbered sections: Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion,
  Acknowledgements.
- 3–6 English keywords.
- Highlights: 3–5 bullets, each ≤85 characters including spaces, as a separate
  editable file.
- Graphical abstract required.
- Numbered Vancouver citations (square brackets), ≤50 references.
- Generative-AI disclosure in a section before the reference list if AI tools
  were used.
- Cover letter must state CMPB fit, what is known, what the study adds, and
  that the paper is unpublished elsewhere.

## Hybrid journal

Subscription publication: no author fee. Open-access APC is approximately
USD 3180 excluding taxes (confirm current amount in the portal).

## Do not dual-submit

The IEEE J-BHI package under `papers/jbhi/` is an archived format of the same
scientific claims. Submit to one journal at a time.
